Understand the distinction.

Revenue is total business sales. Operating surplus subtracts business costs. Cash after debt service subtracts the first year’s scheduled installments for this loan.

Annualized values extend your monthly inputs for 12 months. They are not a growth forecast or measured changes in household income.
