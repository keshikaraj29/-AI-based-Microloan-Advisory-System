06 / Research transparency

Methods, provenance & limitations.

Reproducible analysis

How to read this dashboard

  1. Structured records + narratives

    A reproducible sample joins loan attributes and borrower descriptions from the supplied source data.

  2. Clustering + narrative analysis

    Scaled loan attributes identify borrower groups; text features reveal topics and lexical sentiment.

  3. Evaluation + interpretation

    Silhouette measures cluster separation. Outcomes are compared after grouping; sentiment has no human-labeled accuracy benchmark.

What these data cannot establish

  • Household income before and after a loan is not available.
  • US consumer lending is not a validated proxy for microfinance poverty alleviation.
  • Correlations do not show that loan structure caused an outcome.
