## Before selecting the actual submission

- [ ] Supervisor confirms contribution, suitable venue and whether a student/internal review is the next appropriate step.
- [ ] Authors supply correct names, affiliations, contact details and agreed contribution statements.
- [ ] Team confirms source dataset permissions, redistribution terms, and whether narrative fields contain personal information.
- [ ] Replace an impact-oriented title with an exploratory-systems title unless an impact study is actually completed.
- [ ] Complete an independent evaluation appropriate to the claimed contribution; strengthen the baseline comparisons and report limitations.
- [ ] Describe configurable policy outcomes as review support; do not report automatic bank approval, invented business growth, or manually recorded payments as verified bank settlement.
- [ ] Archive the INR conversion snapshot and explain that display conversion does not change the source population; preserve model reproducibility in original units.
- [ ] Re-run the exact submitted version and archive data hashes, analysis settings, environment, tests, timings and figures.
- [ ] Adapt manuscript length, template, anonymization and citation style to the verified open call.
- [ ] Verify any required funding, ethics, conflict-of-interest, AI-assistance and data-availability declarations with the institution and current venue rules.
- [ ] All authors approve the final text and the designated author performs the submission.
