# Phase 2 review evidence

This folder compares the delivered project with the topic image **PI-15: Analyzing the Impact of Micro-Loans on Poverty Alleviation Using NLP and Clustering** and the supplied excellent-level review rubric. The supplied PP1 Finale is the Phase 1 baseline. The rubric image describes a Phase 2 review; it does not establish that a paper has been submitted or accepted.

## Review order

1. [Topic and rubric audit](topic_and_rubric_audit.md): baseline gaps, delivered evidence, and remaining research requirements.
2. [Research paper draft](research_paper_draft.md): question, methods, results, interpretation, and limitations.
3. [Project plan and risks](project_plan_and_risks.md): baseline comparison, implementation record, dependency chart, and follow-up plan.
4. [SDG and societal impact](sdg_and_societal_impact.md): beneficiary examples and a measurable evaluation proposal.
5. [Presentation and viva](presentation_and_viva.md): slide-ready narrative, demo sequence, and questions with defensible answers.
6. [Submission checklist](submission_checklist.md): venue candidate, author checks, and the external proof still required.
7. [Household outcome guide](household_outcomes_guide.md): validated import/analysis of future baseline/follow-up measurements; no observations fabricated.
8. [Operations and authentication](operations_and_auth.md): account setup, exact policy/FX settings, payment CSV, backup and current deployment limits.
9. [Milk-business assessment example](examples/milk_business_assessment.pdf): a synthetic demonstration using only the supplied story, with missing financial values left unavailable.

Numerical results are in `data/research_summary.json` and provenance is in `data/source_manifest.json`. `docs/test_results.xml` records test outcomes and `docs/performance_results.json` records the measured workload and timings. Those artifacts, together with the source and tests, take precedence over any older screenshot or sample report copied from Phase 1.

## Evidence standard

- A working feature, a measured result, a research inference, and a future activity are different things.
- Supplied agricultural and dairy data provide business context. They do not establish measured loan repayment or a change in household poverty.
- The supplied accepted-loan archive supports exploratory loan-outcome analysis. Its population stays distinct from the Indian microfinance use case even when monetary displays are converted to INR with the disclosed reference snapshot.
- No beneficiary study, human-labeled sentiment benchmark, original team schedule, author list, paper submission, acceptance, or team presentation was supplied. None is invented here.
- The upgrade improves the evidence available for the rubric; it cannot guarantee a grade or make external milestones complete.
